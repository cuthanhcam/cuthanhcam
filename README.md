Two paths always appear before a traveler.

One is wide, familiar, and crowded with footsteps.  
The other is quieter, covered by the shadows of trees, leading somewhere unknown.

The traveler rarely knows which path is right. The only choice is to move forward - to walk, learn, and adapt along the way.

Perhaps the journey is not about finding the perfect path, but about becoming someone capable of walking any path.

Deep within the quiet forest, a small flame remains. It may seem insignificant, yet it carries warmth and light.

Some flames fade with the wind, while others survive the night and illuminate the road ahead.

**Curiosity is the quiet flame that keeps learning, building, and exploring alive, regardless of which path lies ahead.**
